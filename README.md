## Project Overview
Project Atlas is an independent project-management case study demonstrating the end-to-end management of a regulated enterprise application modernization initiative.

The project replaces a legacy business application with a modern web-based platform, integrates the solution with five enterprise systems, migrates required operational and historical data, completes formal validation and user acceptance testing, and transitions the production solution into BAU operations.

The case study demonstrates practical application of predictive project-management practices across initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and control, integrated change control, deployment, stabilization, closure, and post-project benefits governance.

## Business Context
## Business Context
The legacy application had become increasingly costly to maintain and was constrained by aging technology, fragmented workflows, manual controls, and limited integration with enterprise services.

Project Atlas was initiated to modernize the application while maintaining the traceability, validation, security, documentation, and governance expected within a regulated operating environment.

## Project Objectives

The project aimed to:

- Replace the legacy application with a modern, supportable enterprise platform.
- Integrate the solution with five upstream and downstream enterprise systems.
- Migrate required operational and historical data.
- Establish end-to-end traceability between requirements, design, testing, validation, and business acceptance.
- Deliver within the approved Version 1.1 nine-month project window and ₹2.488 crore BAC.
- Achieve production readiness without unresolved Severity-1 defects.
- Complete controlled deployment, hypercare, and transition to operations.
- Transfer post-project benefits measurement to an accountable Business Owner.

## Delivery Approach
Project Atlas follows a predominantly **predictive / Waterfall delivery model** because of defined regulatory requirements, formal validation needs, controlled scope baselines, stage-gate approvals, and coordinated production deployment.

The project lifecycle covered:

**Initiation → Planning → Design → Build → Integration & Testing → Validation & UAT → Deployment → Hypercare → Closure → Benefits Governance**

Formal controls were applied to scope, schedule, cost, resources, quality, risk, procurement, change, release, and benefits management.

## Performance Management Story

Project Atlas intentionally does not present a permanently Green project.

At Month 3, formal Earned Value analysis identified:

- **CPI:** 0.934
- **SPI:** 0.975
- EV below AC
- Cost and schedule pressure requiring corrective action

Following CR-001 approval, the provisional EAC increased to approximately **₹252.00 L** against the revised **₹248.80 L BAC**.

Through corrective action, prioritization, risk response, resource control, vendor management, and objective performance measurement, the project ultimately closed at **₹246.90 L**.

Historical variance remains visible; it was not erased through retrospective rebaselining.

## Production Readiness, Go / No-Go & Deployment

A formal Production Readiness Gate was completed before final production authorization.

The initial outcome was:

**Amber — Conditional Ready**

Three mandatory conditions were closed covering:

- Security retest evidence.
- APAC production-support coverage.
- Final cutover-rehearsal sign-off.

The Steering Committee recorded a formal **GO** decision on **24 August 2026**.

Production deployment completed successfully on **28 August 2026** without rollback.

## Hypercare & Operational Handover

Project Atlas completed a four-week structured hypercare period.

Key stabilization outcomes:

- 17 total hypercare incidents.
- 0 Severity-1 incidents.
- 1 Severity-2 incident, resolved the same business day.
- Approximately 89% reduction in incident volume from Week 1 to Week 4.
- 99.93% availability.
- Five integrations stable.
- 0 open Severity-1 / Severity-2 incidents at exit.

Hypercare exit and BAU operational handover were approved on **25 September 2026**.

## Baseline & Change History

### Baseline Version 1.0
The original Project Atlas baseline included four enterprise integrations, a ₹2.40 crore Cost Baseline / BAC, a ₹0.15 crore Management Reserve held outside BAC, and a planned production Go-Live of 21 August 2026.

### CR-001 — Regulatory Reporting Integration
During project execution and monitoring, a change request was raised to add an Enterprise Regulatory Reporting integration.

CR-001 was assessed across scope, requirements, WBS, schedule, resources, cost, risk, quality and validation, procurement, benefits, and release impact before approval.

The Steering Committee approved CR-001 on 24 April 2026.

### Baseline Version 1.1
Following CR-001 approval:

- Enterprise integration scope increased from four to five integrations.
- Requirements INT-005 and REG-003 were added.
- WBS 4.1.5 — Regulatory Reporting Integration was added.
- The Cost Baseline / BAC increased from ₹2.40 crore to ₹2.488 crore.
- Management Reserve remained unchanged at ₹0.15 crore outside BAC.
- Total Authorized Budget increased from ₹2.55 crore to ₹2.638 crore.
- The approved production Go-Live moved from 21 August 2026 to 28 August 2026.
- Project closure remained 30 September 2026.
- The recurring annual benefit assumption increased from approximately ₹1.30 crore to ₹1.36 crore.

The approved change revised the project baseline without resetting pre-existing execution and cost-performance variance. Historical performance against Baseline Version 1.0 remains traceable through the project change-control records.
