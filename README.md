# Project Atlas

## Regulated Enterprise Application Modernization

**Delivery Model:** Predictive / Waterfall  
**Duration:** 9 Months  
**Core Delivery Team:** 14 Members  
**Workstreams:** 3  
**Cost Baseline (BAC):** ₹2.40 Crore  
**Total Authorized Budget:** ₹2.55 Crore  
**Status:** Portfolio Case Study

## Project Overview
Project Atlas is an independent project-management case study demonstrating the end-to-end management of a regulated enterprise application modernization initiative.

The project involves replacing a legacy business application with a modern web-based platform, integrating the solution with enterprise systems, migrating required data, completing formal validation and user acceptance testing, and deploying the solution across multiple geographic regions.

The case study demonstrates practical application of predictive project-management practices across initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and control, deployment, stabilization, and closure.

## Business Context
The existing application has become increasingly costly to maintain and is constrained by legacy technology, fragmented workflows, manual controls, and limited integration with other enterprise systems.

Project Atlas has been initiated to modernize the application while maintaining the traceability, quality controls, documentation, and governance expected within a regulated operating environment.

## Project Objectives
The project aims to:
- Replace the existing legacy application with a modern, supportable enterprise platform.
- Integrate the solution with four upstream and downstream enterprise systems.
- Migrate required operational and historical data to the new platform.
- Establish end-to-end traceability between requirements, testing, validation, and business acceptance.
- Complete deployment within the approved nine-month schedule and ₹2.40 crore cost baseline.
- Achieve production readiness without unresolved Severity-1 defects.
- Establish structured deployment, hypercare, and transition-to-operations processes.

## Delivery Approach
Project Atlas follows a predominantly **predictive delivery model** because of defined regulatory requirements, formal validation needs, controlled scope baselines, stage-gate approvals, and coordinated production deployment.

The project lifecycle covers:\
**Initiation → Planning → Design → Build → Integration Testing → Validation & UAT → Deployment → Hypercare → Closure**

Formal controls are used for scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk, change, and release management.

## Project Management Areas Demonstrated
This case study will include practical artifacts covering:

**Business & Benefits Management**  
Business justification, benefits, investment assumptions, and project success measures.

**Scope & Requirements**  
Scope baseline, requirements traceability, work breakdown structure, and acceptance criteria.

**Schedule Management**  
Integrated schedule, milestone plan, dependencies, and critical-path monitoring.

**Financial Management**  
Cost estimating, project budget, cost baseline, contingency, management reserve, earned value, forecasting, and variance analysis.

**Resource Management**  
Project organization, responsibility assignment, resource planning, and capacity considerations.

**Risk & Issue Management**  
Risk identification, qualitative assessment, response planning, issue management, escalation, and dependency tracking.

**Quality & Validation**  
Quality planning, validation strategy, testing, defect management, and acceptance.

**Stakeholder & Communication Management**  
Stakeholder analysis, governance forums, reporting, escalation, and executive communication.

**Procurement Management**  
Selected external services, procurement assumptions, and vendor governance.

**Change Control**  
Formal assessment and approval of changes affecting baselined scope, schedule, and cost.

**Release & Transition**  
Release readiness, cutover planning, production deployment, hypercare, and operational handover.

**Project Closure**  
Formal acceptance, lessons learned, financial closure, benefits handover, and project closeout.
