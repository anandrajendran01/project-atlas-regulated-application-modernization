# Project Atlas — Benefits Realization Framework

**Project Closure:** 30 September 2026  
**Benefits Owner:** Business Owner  
**Supporting Functions:** Finance, Operations, Technology / Application Support  
**Approved Recurring Annual Benefit Assumption:** **₹136.00 L per year**  
**Status at Project Closure:** **Handed Over — Realization to be measured post-project**

> Project Atlas is a synthetic portfolio case study. The ₹136.00 L annual benefit is an approved case-study planning assumption, not a claim of realized real-world savings.

---

## 1. Purpose

Project delivery is complete, but benefit realization continues after project closure.

This framework defines how the Business Owner and supporting BAU functions should measure whether Project Atlas delivers the expected operating and financial outcomes after stabilization.

The framework deliberately separates:

- Project delivery performance.
- Post-project benefit realization.
- Financial benefit validation.
- Operational / control improvement.
- Corrective actions if benefits underperform.

---

## 2. Benefits Governance

### Accountable Owner

The **Business Owner** is accountable for benefit realization after project closure.

### Supporting Functions

**Finance**
- Validates financial methodology.
- Confirms realized / annualized benefit assumptions.
- Prevents double counting.
- Reconciles benefit calculations to agreed cost / productivity baselines.

**Operations**
- Tracks process, support, availability, incident, and control metrics.
- Owns corrective operational actions.

**Technology / Application Support**
- Tracks supportability, production stability, platform cost, and technical operating metrics.

**Quality / Compliance / Regulatory Operations**
- Tracks Regulatory Reporting control performance and exception / reconciliation effort where applicable.

## 3. Approved Benefit Position

The approved Version 1.1 recurring annual benefit assumption is:

**₹136.00 L per year**

This includes the original Project Atlas recurring benefit assumption plus the incremental benefit associated with CR-001.

The case study does not allocate the ₹136.00 L into additive sub-benefit values because doing so could create false precision and double counting.

Instead, the financial benefit is tracked as one consolidated run-rate measure supported by operating KPIs.

---

## 4. Benefit Measures

### BEN-001 — Recurring Financial Benefit

**Target:** ₹136.00 L annualized run-rate  
**Owner:** Business Owner / Finance  
**Method:** Finance-validated comparison of the agreed post-project cost / productivity position against the approved reference baseline.

### BEN-002 — Legacy Support / Maintenance Reduction

**Direction:** Reduction versus legacy operating baseline  
**Owner:** Technology / Finance  
**Method:** Compare application support, maintenance, infrastructure, and specialist-effort trends.

### BEN-003 — Manual Workflow Effort Reduction

**Direction:** Material reduction  
**Owner:** Business Operations  
**Method:** Compare representative process effort / handling time against the legacy baseline.

### BEN-004 — Regulatory Reporting Reconciliation Reduction

**Direction:** Material reduction  
**Owner:** Business / Compliance Operations  
**Method:** Track manual reporting, reconciliation, exception handling, and reprocessing effort.

### BEN-005 — Process Cycle-Time Improvement

**Direction:** Improvement versus legacy baseline  
**Owner:** Business Owner  
**Method:** Track elapsed time for selected priority workflows.

### BEN-006 — Production Availability

**Target:** ≥99.5%  
**Owner:** Operations  
**Method:** Service-monitoring data.

### BEN-007 — Regulatory Reporting Acknowledgement Success

**Target:** ≥99.5%  
**Owner:** Operations / Regulatory Reporting Platform Owner  
**Method:** Integration-monitoring data.

### BEN-008 — Support Incident Trend

**Target:** Stable / declining after stabilization  
**Owner:** Operations  
**Method:** Incident and problem-management data.

## 5. Review Cadence

### 3-Month Post-Stabilization Review — 31 December 2026

Primary purpose:

- Confirm adoption and operating stability.
- Establish the first post-project financial run-rate estimate.
- Check whether key process / support KPIs are moving in the expected direction.
- Identify benefit-realization blockers early.

### 6-Month Benefits Review — 31 March 2027

Primary purpose:

- Validate financial benefit run-rate with Finance.
- Confirm sustained operational improvement.
- Assess whether corrective actions are producing expected results.
- Update the benefits forecast transparently.

### 12-Month Benefits Review — 30 September 2027

Primary purpose:

- Confirm annualized benefit realization.
- Reconcile realized benefit against the ₹136.00 L assumption.
- Record residual gaps / additional opportunities.
- Formally close the benefits-measurement cycle or transition it into normal business-performance governance.

---

## 6. Financial Validation Principles

Benefit reporting should:

1. Use an agreed reference baseline.
2. Avoid double counting the same saving in multiple categories.
3. Distinguish hard financial savings from productivity / capacity benefits.
4. Distinguish realized benefit from forecast benefit.
5. Exclude project-delivery cost variance from benefit-realization calculations.
6. Document material changes to assumptions.
7. Use Finance validation before presenting realized financial benefit as confirmed.
8. Avoid attributing unrelated business changes to Project Atlas.

---

## 7. Benefit Status Rules

**Green**
- Financial / operational measures are on track.
- No material corrective action required.

**Amber**
- Benefit gap exists but recovery action is credible and owned.

**Red**
- Material underperformance exists without an adequate recovery path or benefit assumption requires formal revision.

**Pending**
- Review date has not yet occurred or required measurement evidence is not yet available.

---

## 8. Corrective Action Process

If a material benefit gap is identified:

1. Quantify the gap.
2. Identify whether the cause is adoption, operating model, process, cost, technical performance, or measurement methodology.
3. Assign an accountable owner.
4. Define action, due date, and expected benefit impact.
5. Reforecast the benefit transparently.
6. Review progress at the next benefits-governance checkpoint.
7. Escalate material assumption changes to the Business Owner and Finance.

---

## 9. Relationship to Project Closure

Project Atlas closed successfully on 30 September 2026.

The project closure decision confirms delivery and transition; it does **not** imply that the full ₹136.00 L annual benefit has already been realized.

Benefits ownership therefore continues after project closure under BAU governance.
