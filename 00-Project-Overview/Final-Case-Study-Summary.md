# Project Atlas — Final Case Study Summary

## Regulated Enterprise Application Modernization

Project Atlas is an independent end-to-end project-management portfolio case study demonstrating the management of a regulated enterprise application modernization initiative using a predominantly predictive / Waterfall delivery model.

The case study follows the project from business justification and chartering through scope, schedule, cost, resource, risk, quality, procurement, execution control, formal change management, production readiness, deployment, hypercare, closure, and post-project benefits governance.

## Business Situation

The legacy application was increasingly difficult and expensive to support and was constrained by fragmented workflows, manual controls, limited integration, and an aging technology base.

Project Atlas was initiated to modernize the solution while preserving the governance, validation, traceability, security, and controlled-release practices required in a regulated enterprise environment.

---

## Delivery Strategy

A predictive approach was selected because the case involves:

- Formal requirements baselines.
- Validation evidence.
- Stage-gate governance.
- Controlled scope / cost / schedule baselines.
- Enterprise dependencies.
- Data migration.
- Formal production authorization.
- Structured cutover and stabilization.

The delivery lifecycle was:

**Initiation → Planning → Design → Build → Integration / Testing → Validation & UAT → Deployment → Hypercare → Closure → Benefits Governance**

---

## Key Management Challenge — Performance Pressure

Month-3 performance showed that apparent underspend did not represent true project efficiency.

EVM identified:

- CPI: **0.934**
- SPI: **0.975**
- EV below AC
- Cost and schedule pressure requiring corrective action

This case therefore demonstrates objective performance management rather than a permanently Green project narrative.

---

## Key Management Challenge — CR-001

During execution, CR-001 proposed a fifth enterprise integration:

**Enterprise Regulatory Reporting Integration**

The change was not immediately added to the baseline.

The case demonstrates:

1. Formal change request.
2. Integrated impact analysis.
3. Option evaluation.
4. Steering Committee decision.
5. Funding authorization.
6. Baseline revision.
7. Continued variance visibility.

CR-001 added:

- INT-005
- REG-003
- WBS 4.1.5
- ₹8.80 L incremental BAC
- A five-working-day Go-Live shift

The baseline moved from Version 1.0 to Version 1.1 without resetting pre-existing project variance.

---

## Production Readiness & Release

Before final production authorization, the project completed a formal readiness gate.

Initial outcome:

**Amber — Conditional Ready**

Three mandatory conditions were then closed:

- Security retest evidence.
- APAC support coverage.
- Final cutover-rehearsal sign-off.

The Steering Committee recorded a formal **GO** decision on 24 August 2026.

Production deployment completed successfully on 28 August 2026 without rollback.

---

## Hypercare & Operational Transition

Project Atlas completed four weeks of structured hypercare.

Key results:

- 17 total hypercare incidents.
- 0 Sev-1.
- 1 Sev-2, resolved the same business day.
- ~89% reduction in weekly incident volume from Week 1 to Week 4.
- 99.93% availability.
- 0 open Sev-1 / Sev-2 at exit.
- Five integrations stable.
- BAU handover completed.

Hypercare exit was approved on 25 September 2026.
